Store Fix

Keep App Store links in your chosen region.

Enable in Settings

You can turn on Store Fix's Safari extension in Settings.

Checking Safari extension status

You can enable Store Fix from Safari Extensions preferences.

Safari extension is on

Use the Safari toolbar popup to choose your fixed region.

Safari extension is off

Turn on Store Fix in Safari Extensions preferences to start fixing App Store links.

After enabling Store Fix, manage regions from the Safari toolbar popup.

After enabling Store Fix, manage regions from Safari.
